## Photographing the lightning

- Use a tripod on the stilt-house deck. On a boat, photos blur.
- Focus manually, at infinity. Autofocus fails in the dark.
- Expose for 10–30 s at ISO 800–3200 and f/2.8–f/5.6. Shoot continuously and keep the best frames.
- On a phone, use night mode and prop it against something firm.
- Turn the flash off and set your head torch to red. Your neighbours are watching too.
- Put the camera back in the dry bag when the rain comes.
