## Health

South of the lake it is hot and very humid, at 22–32 °C. Drink often. Mosquitoes come out at dusk.

See a travel clinic a few weeks before you go and ask about yellow fever vaccination and malaria prevention for the area. Do not drink or swim in the lake water, because parts of the lake are polluted.

Ologá and Congo Mirador have only a basic health post, with few staff and little medicine. An emergency means a long boat ride. Bring a first-aid kit and your own medicines.

## Safety

Follow weather warnings and the advice of local guides. Do not travel by boat during a storm or shelter under isolated trees. Emergencies: **VEN 911**.

On open water a boat is the highest point around. If you can hear thunder, the storm is close enough to strike. Get off the water and under a roof. Wear your life jacket on every ride.

Check your government's travel advice before you book. Several countries advise against travel to Venezuela or to areas near the Colombian border. The Catatumbo river flows down from that border, and in the past irregular armed groups were active on the river and the lake. Ask your operator about the current situation. Take out travel insurance that covers Venezuela.
