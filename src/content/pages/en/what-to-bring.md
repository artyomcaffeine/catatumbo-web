key: packing
title: What to bring
description: What you need for a night in a stilt house, with mosquitoes, rain, sun and little power.
group: tips
order: 2
---

Insect repellent, long sleeves, a rain jacket, a head torch, cash, drinking water and a dry bag for your phone and camera. Electricity and signal are limited in the stilt villages.

Also bring a hat and sunscreen for the boat ride, light quick-drying clothes, a power bank, snacks and any medicine you need. Power usually comes from a generator, and only for a few hours.
