## A typical trip

Itineraries vary. A two-day trip often looks like this:

- On the first morning, an early start from Mérida or a pick-up at El Vigía airport, then the drive down to Puerto Concha.
- In the afternoon, a boat ride through the channels and mangroves of the national park, with herons, egrets and other water birds along the way. Arrival at Ologá before sunset.
- At night, dinner, often fish from the lake. Then the storm, watched from the deck or from the boat at a safe distance. You sleep in a hammock or a bed in a stilt house.
- On the second day, sunrise over the lake, the boat back to the landing and the drive home in the afternoon.

Three-day trips add more time on the water, a second night for the lightning, or a stop in the Andes.
